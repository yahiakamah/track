Overview

The Odoo 17 Full Accounting Kit for the Community Edition brings essential accounting features that are available in the Enterprise Edition to the free Community Edition. It is an ideal solution for small to medium-sized businesses that need comprehensive accounting functionalities without upgrading to Enterprise. This kit enhances the Community Edition by providing key accounting tools, ensuring users can manage their finances efficiently.
