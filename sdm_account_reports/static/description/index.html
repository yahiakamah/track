Overview

Odoo Accounting offers a comprehensive suite of financial reports that provide real-time insights into a business's financial health. Key reports include the Profit & Loss Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, and General Ledger, which help track revenues, expenses, assets, liabilities, and overall financial performance. Other important reports, such as Aged Receivables, Aged Payables, and Tax Reports, assist in managing outstanding invoices, vendor bills, and tax compliance. Customizable and easy to generate, these reports ensure efficient financial management and informed decision-making.
